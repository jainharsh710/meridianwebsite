The rules, simply.

How much can I invest abroad?Up to $250,000 per person, per financial year.
This is the RBI’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme. Each family member has their own limit, so a family of four can remit up to $1 million a year.
Is there tax when I send money?20% TCS on remittances above ₹10 lakh a year.
Tax collected at source is an advance, not an extra tax. You adjust it against your income tax when you file. Calculate your TCS →
How are my gains taxed?12.5% after 24 months; your slab rate before that.
Gains on foreign shares and ETFs held for more than 24 months are long-term and taxed at 12.5%, plus surcharge and cess.
What about dividends?Taxed in India, with credit for US tax withheld.
The US withholds 25% on dividends for Indian residents under the tax treaty, which you claim as a foreign tax credit in India.
What do I have to report?Schedule FA in your income tax return.
Foreign assets are disclosed every year. We give you a tax-ready summary for your CA.
Is there US estate tax?Yes, above $60,000 in US assets.
Non-US persons holding US-located assets above $60,000 at death can face US estate tax. Check your exposure →
