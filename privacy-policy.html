Privacy Policy

Draft for legal review · Last updated October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Meridian Capital collects, why, and your rights, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Details you give us through our website forms: name, email, phone, city and the investment range you select.
  • Information needed to provide our services after you sign an agreement, such as identity details, financial information and your risk profile.
  • Basic technical data from your visit, such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location, collected through Google Analytics.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry.
  • To provide our services under a signed agreement.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including record-keeping.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, under confidentiality obligations: Web3Forms, which delivers our contact form to us; Google Analytics, which measures site usage; and Cloudflare, which hosts the site and routes our email. We also share it with authorities where the law requires.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site, for example which pages are read and how people find us. It sets cookies in your browser and processes data on Google’s servers, which may be outside India. We do not use advertising cookies or sell this data. You can block cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

How long we keep it

Enquiry data is kept for up to 24 months. Client records are kept for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to exercise your rights. Write to privacy@meridiancapital.in. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.
