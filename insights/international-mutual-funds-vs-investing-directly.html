International mutual funds vs investing directly.

Why the easy route has mostly closed, and what the direct route costs.

By Harsh Jain & Harsh Bothra · October 2026 · 5 min read

54 of 66International funds closed to fresh money, July 2026
$7BIndustry-wide cap on overseas investment
$250,000LRS limit per person, per year

For years, the simplest way for an Indian to invest abroad was an Indian mutual fund that did it for you: a US feeder fund or an international fund of funds, bought like any other fund, in rupees, with no paperwork. For most investors, that door is now shut.

Why the funds closed

Indian mutual funds can invest overseas only within an industry-wide limit set by the RBI and SEBI: $7 billion, plus a separate $1 billion for overseas ETFs. Once the industry reached it, fund houses stopped taking new money. As of July 2026, Value Research tracked 66 international funds, and 54 were not accepting fresh investments. The few still open cap new SIPs or lump sums, some at ₹5,000 a month.

The limit has been raised before and may be again. But a strategy that depends on a regulatory cap staying open is fragile.

How the two routes compare

Indian international fundDirect, under LRS
AvailabilityMostly closed to new moneyOpen, up to $250,000 a year per person
ChoiceA few dozen fundsThousands of global funds and shares
CostsTwo layers: the Indian fund plus the underlying fundOne layer: the fund you own
Tax on gains12.5% after 24 months12.5% after 24 months
TCSNone20% above ₹10 lakh a year, adjustable against tax
PaperworkNone extraSchedule FA and Form 67 in your return
US estate taxNot applicableAvoidable with Irish-domiciled funds

When each makes sense

If you want a small global allocation and can still find an open fund, the Indian fund route is simple. Its tax treatment is now the same as investing directly for units held more than 24 months.

For anyone building a meaningful global portfolio, the direct route wins on availability, choice and cost. The extra work is real, but it is a few schedules in your tax return, not a burden through the year. It is also exactly the part we take off your hands.
