The problem

Your HNIs are already investing abroad. Just not through you.

Clients who have built wealth in Indian markets are now opening US accounts on apps, often after one conversation with a friend. Each one is AUM that leaves your book, and a relationship that starts to drift.

Most distributors don’t offer global because the work is unfamiliar: LRS limits, TCS, Form A2, US estate tax, Schedule FA at filing time. That is the work we take off your desk.

Questions

Is there an empanelment fee?
No. There is no fee to empanel and no minimum business commitment.
Who owns the client relationship?
You do. Clients are introduced as yours, and you can see their accounts in your partner view. If a client contacts us directly, we bring you into the conversation.
Does my client pay more because they came through me?
No. Every client pays the same fee schedule, whether they come to us directly or through a partner.
What is the minimum investment for a client?
$5,000 per client introduced through a partner.
